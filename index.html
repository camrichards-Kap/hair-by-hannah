Meet Hannah

Hair that grows out as good as it looks on day one.

Hi, I’m Hannah, an Orange County hairstylist behind the chair at Salon Zinnia in San Clemente.

Blonding is my favorite thing to do: bright, buttery, or a soft bronde, always placed so it grows out gently and you’re not stuck in the salon every six weeks. I love a good haircut too, from face-framing layers to a big chop into a bob.

Every appointment starts with a conversation about your hair, your routine and your budget, so we land on something that fits your real life. All are welcome in my chair, including men’s cuts and kids.

Hannah
